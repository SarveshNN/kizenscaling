Straight answers.

What does it cost?+

A few thousand dollars all in to put one new pool on your schedule — Google spend plus what you pay me. The exact number depends on your area, so we work it out on the call.

How is this different from Angi or HomeAdvisor?+

Those platforms sell the same homeowner to several builders at once. Google Local Services Ads sends the call to one business — yours. You aren't racing for a lead you already paid for.

I'm already running Google Ads. Does this replace them?+

No. Local Services Ads sit in a separate block above the standard paid ads. Most builders run both, and LSA usually produces a lower cost per booked consultation.

What do you need from me to get started?+

Your licence and insurance for Google's verification, access to your Google Business Profile, real photos of your crew and finished work, and a number to route calls to. The tedious application is on me.

How fast will the phone ring?+

Verified and live inside 30 days, first consultation on your calendar inside 45. If not, you get your setup fee back.

What does "one contractor per area" mean?+

I only run one business per trade in a given service area, so I'm never bidding against my own client.
